About Ethical Hacking Hub

Ethical Hacking Hub (E.H.H.) is a learning platform for people who want to understand cybersecurity from the inside: how systems are attacked, so they can be defended properly.

The platform brings together learning paths for network security, web exploitation, penetration testing, malware analysis, digital forensics, cryptography, programming, Nmap and network scanning, Linux, OSINT, cloud security, and incident response. Admins can add learning materials to each path, so you can study each subject in one place.

Our approach

Everything on E.H.H. is meant for authorized, legal practice: your own lab environments, deliberately vulnerable practice systems, and sanctioned assessments. Understanding offense is a means to a defensive end, not an excuse to target systems you don't have permission to test.

What you'll find here

A dashboard of learning paths with downloadable materials added over time, and a curated list of Linux distributions built for security work, so you can get your own toolkit running quickly.
